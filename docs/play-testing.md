# Google Play closed testing

Drift on Google Play is **closed testing**. There is no public listing yet.

1. Join the [CutWire Drift testers](https://groups.google.com/g/cutwire-drift-testers) Google Group with the Google account you use on the Play Store.
2. With that same account, open the [testing opt-in](https://play.google.com/apps/testing/org.cutwire.drift) and become a tester.

After that, Drift appears in Play Store for that account, and you can install it from there.
